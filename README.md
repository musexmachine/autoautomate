# autoautomate

**Find the work you repeat. Decide what a computer should do for you.**

Anyone who uses a computer has tasks they do again and again without noticing. autoautomate notices them for you. It watches how you work, finds your most repeated tasks, and shows you which ones are worth automating. Then you decide.

## Goals

- **Anyone can use it.** You don't need to code. If you use a computer, it works for you.
- **It runs on your computer.** Your activity data stays on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you publish a summary yourself.
- **It shows you what's real.** It ranks tasks by the time you actually spend on them, not by what you remember spending.
- **You stay in charge.** Every task gets a confidence score and a reason. You label each one "candidate for automation" or "keep human."
- **It turns patterns into ideas.** It ranks your best candidates as automation or product ideas, and explains why each one ranks where it does.
- **It's yours to switch on and off.** Every data source is off until you turn it on. You always know where your data lives.

## How it works

1. **Watch.** Turn on the sources you want, like apps, browser history or clipboard.
2. **Find.** autoautomate groups your activity into repeated tasks.
3. **Score.** Each task gets minutes per week and a confidence that it can be automated.
4. **Decide.** You label each task.
5. **Share (optional).** Publish an anonymized summary as a link that reads well on any screen.

## Platforms

macOS. The menu bar app (Swift) is on the Mac App Store. The optional autoautomate Helper (Go) adds advanced sources and is a free download.
